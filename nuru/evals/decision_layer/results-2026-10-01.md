## What changed

- **Better, on judgment:** test 2 finds the correction note (600 s keepalive) every time; 27
  pushes back that a parsec is a distance; 21 and 22 get further into the right notes. Tests 1, 5,
  8, 11, 12, 16, 18 and 23 also edge up.
- **Worse, on speed:** tests 7 and 9 time out. The 8B thinks at about 14 tokens/s, and the watch's
  "1,200-token" budget (estimated at four characters a token, which undercounts numeric thinking)
  meant 231-292 s of thinking per stop on test 7, against a 15-minute limit. Test 32 is slow too
  (7-11 tool calls, 620 s a run).
- **Worse, on choosing tools:** test 20 calls `find_broken_links` for "contradictions" (0.4); test
  31 writes the standup into today's daily note with `add_to_daily` instead of creating a
  transcript note, in all five runs (1/5 each).
- **Unchanged:** 13 and 25 never pass on either model.

The 8B's gains are judgment; its losses are the watch's budget on a slower model and tool choice.
Both are things to fix in Nuru (a time- or real-token budget; the tools purge), so the 3B stays the
default and the 8B is worth re-testing after the purge.

## For training Laya (`laya_metrics.py`)

A new eval script, run after `autolabel.py` on one or two batches: label yield and balance per
question, Laya's zero-shot accuracy, calibration (ECE) and confusion, its confident mistakes
(wrong at p >= 0.7, by decision id - the most useful training examples), each watch stop against
whether its run passed, and the two models' tool paths per test.

`autolabel.py` added 603 labels from the 8B batches (585 + 12 + 6), about 3,900 in all.

| Question | Laya's accuracy, 3B batch | 8B batch | Labels (8B) | Confident mistakes (8B) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tool_gate start (read / write / answer) | 12% | 13% | 158 (read 138, write 20) | 55 |
| tool_gate next | 55% | 49% | 288 | 0 |
| check_claim | 69% | 65% | 124 | 17 |
| gate_write verdict | 98% | 67% | 15 (all proceed) | 0 |

- Laya's accuracy hardly depends on the model it watches, so training should carry over between
  models. gate_write is the exception: it hesitated on a third of the 8B's legitimate writes - and
  it still has no negative examples.
- Watch stops come mostly in runs that fail (25 of the 8B's 31 budget stops, 10 of the 3B's 13): they
  mark hard tests, so a stop alone isn't a label for "stopping helped".
- The two models' first tool differs on 13 tests (5, 7, 9, 12, 13, 20, 21, 25, 26, 27, 31 and, for
  lack of runs in the main batch, 33 and 34; 7 and 9 because they timed out before a call was
  logged). These are candidates for strategy labels from the expert teacher.
